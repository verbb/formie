Build polished forms in Craft with a visual builder, more than 30 field types, intelligent conditions, flexible notifications and deep integration support.

Formie covers the full form workflow: authors design single or multi-page forms, visitors submit through accessible project-owned templates, teams manage submissions in Craft and integrations carry useful data into the services around the site.

## Features

- Drag-and-drop form builder with rows, columns and multi-page forms.
- More than 30 standard, advanced and compound field types.
- Conditions for pages, fields, buttons, notifications and integrations.
- Synced fields, stencils and form templates for reusable configuration.
- Multiple email notifications with variable pickers and conditional recipients.
- Custom email templates, uploaded-file and PDF attachments, previews and test delivery.
- Searchable Craft submission elements with statuses, exports and bulk actions.
- Page-reload or asynchronous submissions, redirects and saved incomplete submissions.
- Content encryption, submission-retention rules and configurable spam protection.
- Payment integrations for one-off and provider-supported recurring payments.
- Accessible bundled form templates with complete Twig override support.
- Component-level theming for tags, attributes and utility CSS classes.
- CRM, email marketing, automation, webhook, captcha and address integrations.
- GraphQL queries and mutations for headless form experiences.
- JSON form import and export, Feed Me support and migration assistants for Freeform and Sprout Forms.
