# Note

Use Note when you want to leave guidance for people editing the form in the form builder.

Notes are builder-only. They appear in the form builder layout, but are not rendered on the front-end form, in submissions, or in email summaries.

## Key settings

- **Note** — The message shown in the form builder.
- **Style** — Choose Tip, Warning, Info, or Error styling to match the tone of the guidance.

## Submitted value

Note fields do not save a submitted value and are excluded from front-end rendering.

## Related fields

- Use [Rich Text](/fields/content) for formatted content visible to form users.
- Use [HTML](/fields/html) for developer-authored markup and Twig on the front-end form.
- Use [Heading](/fields/heading) for simple section titles shown to form users.
