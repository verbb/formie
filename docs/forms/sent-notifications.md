# Sent Notifications

Sent notifications are the record of emails Formie has already sent.

Use sent notifications when you need to confirm what was sent, inspect the delivered content, or resend an email without creating a new submission.

You can use them to check whether a notification was generated at all, see the exact content that was sent, confirm who received it, and resend one message or several messages in bulk. They are separate from [Email Notifications](/forms/email-notifications), which control what will be sent in future.

## Resending

If emails are failing broadly, check your mailer and queue setup first.

Resending is useful for individual cases. It is not a substitute for fixing a delivery problem at the source.
